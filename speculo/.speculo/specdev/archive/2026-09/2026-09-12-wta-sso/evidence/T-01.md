## What the tests drive (shipped code)

- `SsoAppRegistration.prepare` (called by `SysSsoAppController` add/update): forces `ssoEnabled=true`, defaults kind/authMode/PKCE, rejects empty redirects and `*`.
- `SsoRedirectUris.validateExact` / `validateOne`: wildcard and unlisted URI rejected.
- `SsoClientFieldsSupport.apply`: public own-app persists exact redirect without storing plaintext; confidential hashes secret and returns plaintext once; `fillView` never emits hash.
- `SysClientServiceImpl.insertByBo`: wildcard / illegal URI throw `ServiceException` **before** persistence (`SysClientServiceSsoUnitTest`).
- Domain `createSystemService().ssoApps`: GET `/system/ssoApp/list|{id}`, POST `/system/ssoApp`, `/update`, `/rotateSecret` (not `/system/client`).
- `SsoAppPage.vue` + `createSystemWebDomain`: componentKey `system/ssoApp/index`, title `sso-admin-title`, buttons 创建应用 / 拿配置, uses `runtime.service.ssoApps`, does not call `runtime.service.clients`.
