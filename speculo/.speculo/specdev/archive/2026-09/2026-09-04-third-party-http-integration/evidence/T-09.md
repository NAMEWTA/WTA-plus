## Implementation

Every physical gateway send enters `ThirdInvocationRecorderAdapter` through the default `ThirdInvocationRecorderPort.recordAttempt` hook before and after transport, while one logical result still records invocation detail and aggregates. Invocation detail stores sanitized request/response summaries and attempt count; statistics retain provider and endpoint aggregates with success, failure, timeout, rejected, and quota fields. `ThirdLogSanitizerAdapter` enforces an immutable sensitive-key blacklist, endpoint-additive redaction, valid bounded JSON values, and a UTF-8-safe 16 KiB cap before writing to the existing `SysLogEventSink`. A dedicated actuator health indicator exposes sink failure count. Recorder persistence and sink failures are best effort and do not replace the synchronous gateway result.
