## Remaining test-hygiene and evidence limits

- In hold tests 8, 9 and 13, `CompletableFuture.runAsync(worker::poll)` is only awaited on the normal path (test lines 388-397, 411-428, 521-533). If the hold/DB/status assertion fails first, `finally` calls `/disarm` without bounded join/cancel of that worker. A prior worker could cross into a subsequent ordered case and contaminate S3 counts or captured mail. A failure-path cleanup should release/cancel the exact gate and await this future with a finite bound, without masking the original assertion. Runner's final container/process cleanup does not establish per-method isolation.
- The proxy accepts at most 2 MiB and uses socket 10-second inactivity timeouts, not an absolute body-read wall-clock cap (proxy lines 24, 135-150). The test's synthetic source bytes are tiny. After `/release-hold`, already released waiters can still forward; final `cancel_holds` blocks new retries but cannot retract a forwarded PUT. Acceptance requires the proxy drained count and real target-byte assertions, not an assumption that cancellation undoes provider effects.
